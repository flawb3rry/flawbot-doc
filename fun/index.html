    Fun

    acceptbattle

    Accept a battle when you were challenged.

    !acceptbattle

    Input:

    !acceptbattle
    Output: 
    {username} launches an epic strike on flawb3rry and emerges victorious! 🏆

    battle

    Challenge another user to a battle.

    !battle <username>

    Input:

    !battle flawb3rry
    Output: 
    {username} has challenged flawb3rry to a duel! Type !acceptbattle to join the battle!

    dailyfact

    Get the daily fact.

    !dailyfact

    Input:

    !dailyfact
    Output: 
    Todays fact: Cats are cool.

    endriot

    Ends a riot.

    !endriot

    Input:

     !endriot
    Output: 
    {username} ended the riot.

    riot

    Starts or joins a riot.

    !riot

    Input:

     !riot
    Output: 
    {username} started a riot!

    hug

    Hug another user.

    !hug <username>

    Input:

     !hug flawb3rry
    Output: 
    {username} gives flawb3rry a hug!
